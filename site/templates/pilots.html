New Race-day HDZero firmware

Change channel on the goggles.
The quad follows.

Free custom firmware for HDZero goggles and their ELRS backpack. Pick a channel on the goggles and the drone's VTX, your radio and the race timer all retune with you. And the timer can put every pilot on their race channel, Lowband included.

Goggles drive the quad

Dial to a channel and confirm: the backpack sends it to your drone's VTX, your radio's VTX Administrator and the timer. Turn on Auto Send VTX to follow every change, or long-press to send (and re-send) on demand. A VTX SENT pill confirms it on screen.

Every band, Lowband too

The race director sets a pilot to Raceband or L1–L8 from dd-pits and the goggles switch band and tune. Stock firmware can't take a Lowband channel from the timer. The race-day builds also let the dial reach every band.

DVR named after the heat

dd-pits labels each pilot's next recording as their heat is cued, and the goggles add it to the usual name: hdz_0042-WinterCup-Mains-A-R5-H1.mp4. Find any race in seconds. Turn it on once: Record Option → Naming Scheme → ELRS.

The right time, always

The goggle clock is set from the timer over the backpack, so every recording carries the correct date and time.

Nothing taken away

Stock HDZero firmware with the race-day features added on top. Covers HDZero Goggle, Goggle 2 and BoxPro. The DVR and Lowband changes are submitted upstream to HDZero.

Two files, flashed as a pair

One for the goggles (Goggle, Goggle 2 or BoxPro), one for the goggle backpack.
