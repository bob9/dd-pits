Race calls

One voice.
Right on the lap.

Laps, holeshots, results and "next up" — spoken by dd-pits instead of FPVTrackside's own speech, in your club's own wording.

Your club's wording

Calls are read from FPVTrackside's own Sounds.xml, so the phrasing and on/off switches you set there carry over. dd-pits finds FPVTrackside's files itself, and an audit flags settings that disagree.

One switch decides who speaks

dd-pits sets FPVTrackside's speech volume to match, so you never hear both — or neither. No voice set up? FPVTrackside keeps speaking and dd-pits stays out of the audio.

Fast calls

dd-pits listens for FPVTrackside's live events as well as polling it. Voice packs of pre-generated clips make common calls play instantly.

Race director controls

Hurry Up, Announce Race and the other calls FPVTrackside binds to keyboard shortcuts — trigger them from the laptop or any phone at the field.

Pronunciations

Tell the voice how to say a tricky callsign without changing how it's spelled anywhere else.

Clean hand-offs

When the operator moves to the next race, anything still waiting to be said about the last one is dropped.

RotorHazard 4.0 or later

Install the plugin

  1. Upload the zip at Settings → Plugins (4.3+), or copy dd_pits into src/server/plugins.
  2. Restart RotorHazard. dd-pits appears in its menu.
  3. Open dd-pits → Brackets → Create bracket.

Back FPVTrackside

Run it beside the timer

  1. Download dd-pits for your laptop and run it next to the FPVTrackside alpha (brackets need its REST API).
  2. Open Brackets → Create bracket at localhost:8088.
  3. Every race goes on FPVTrackside round by round, with pilots and channels set. dd-pits starts them and reads the results back.
  4. Need to change something by hand? Race director → Meeting editor edits rounds, races, line-ups and pilots in FPVTrackside.

Marshalling · RotorHazard timers

Every lap.
Exactly right.

dd-pits reads each pilot's signal from your RotorHazard timer, finds the enter and exit lines for you, and points at the laps that look wrong, with the fix one tap away. Corrections go back through RotorHazard's own marshal save, so results, brackets and FPVTrackside all follow.

dd-pits · Marshal

Lines found for you

Auto-detect measures the background and the passes and picks the enter and exit lines that give steady, believable laps, then tells you how sure it is and why.

Problems pointed out

Double counts, stray signals, missed passes (with the weak peak where it probably was), passes only just over the line, and too few laps for the race.

Bleed spotted

Every pilot on one time axis. A weak bump that lines up with another pilot's strong pass is called out as their signal bleeding across, not a lap.

Drag, tap, done

Drag the lines and the laps recalculate as you go, by RotorHazard's own rule. Tap a pass to drop it, tap the trace to add one. From the laptop or a phone at the field.

Saved the RotorHazard way

Corrections save through RotorHazard's own marshal save, and Use these lines from now on sets the node for the races to come.

FPVTrackside too

When FPVTrackside times with a RotorHazard, dd-pits finds the timer in FPVTrackside's settings and marshals from its signal. Corrections land back in FPVTrackside.
